Privacy Policy

Design and Craft Mart. Last updated: October 2026.

Design and Craft Mart is a small business in Bangladesh that designs and sells photo designs and frames, business cards and logos. This page explains what information we collect from customers who use our website, why we collect it, and what we do with it.

What we collect

  • When you sign in with Google: your name, your email address and your Google profile picture. We do not receive your Google password, and we do not read your emails, contacts or files.
  • When you send an order request: your name, phone number, the product you want, your description of it, and the date you need it (if you give one).
  • When you ask the chat assistant a question: the messages you type in the chat.

Why we use it

  • To let you sign in and see your own order requests and their status.
  • To contact you about your request, agree the price and delivery date, and confirm payment.
  • To answer your questions and improve our service.

Who can see it

Only you and the approved members of the Design and Craft Mart team can see your requests. We do not sell your information and we do not share it with advertisers. Our website is hosted with Vercel, our data is stored with Supabase, and chat answers are prepared with the help of Anthropic's Claude. These services process data on our behalf to run the website.

How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as you have an account or an active order, and for a reasonable time afterwards for our business records.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to show you the information we hold about you, correct it, or delete your account and your requests. Email us at the address below and we will respond as soon as we can.

Payments

We do not collect card numbers on this website. Payments are made directly by bKash, Nagad or bank transfer, and we record only the method and the transaction reference you send us.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.
